Welcome to UP Diliman


UP Diliman or the University of the Philippines Diliman is a public research university located in Quezon City.

One of the well-known architectural pieces within the campus is The Oblation. The Oblation is a statue that is an iconic figure of the UP System. It was created by National Artist Guillermo Tolentino in 1935.
